Envelope05
Business model

Gas that pays for itself.

  • 0.5% fee, paid by the sender on top. The recipient always gets exactly what was promised.
  • The fee lands in an on-chain gas fund. The relayer is paid back automatically, per transaction. No outside sponsor, no admin keys.
  • On Mezo a claim costs a tiny fraction of a cent in BTC gas. The rest is revenue.
  • Next: off-ramp revenue share from partners who turn MUSD into naira, cedis and shillings.

Envelope08
Built safely
  • EIP-712 signatures bound to the contract, chain, envelope and recipient. Nobody can redirect a claim.
  • The link key lives only in the URL fragment, never sent to a server.
  • No owner, no admin keys. Fee capped in code. Surplus can only go to the fixed treasury.
  • Loan-health guard refuses risky borrows.
