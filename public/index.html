Questions

Before you send

What is MUSD?

MUSD is Mezo's dollar, backed by Bitcoin and designed to stay worth $1. This demo runs on Mezo testnet, so the MUSD here is test money with no cash value.

What if they never claim it?

After 7 days the envelope can be refunded, and the refund always goes back to you. Anyone can trigger it, but nobody can send it anywhere else.

Do they need a wallet or an app?

No. When they tap Claim, a wallet is made for them in their browser. No app, no password, no sign-up. They can move the money to their own wallet later.

Who pays the network fee?

You pay 0.5% on top of what you send. That fee goes into a gas fund inside the contract, which pays our relayer back for the network fee. The person you send to receives every cent.

What is the risk of paying from my Bitcoin?

You borrow MUSD against your BTC on Mezo, and Mezo adds a 0.1% borrowing fee to your loan. If the BTC price falls and your loan drops below Mezo's 110% line, your Bitcoin can be sold to repay it. We show your loan health before and after, and we block any send that would take you below 120%.

Can someone else take the money?

Anyone who has the link can claim it, like cash in an envelope. Only share it with the person it's for.
